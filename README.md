## What I'm Building

I'm the founder and developer of **[Zynkbot](https://github.com/MSkill1/zynkbot)**, an open-source AI assistant that runs entirely on your own hardware. No cloud. No surveillance. No data leaving your device.

Zynkbot is built on a privacy-first architecture: local LLM inference, on-device semantic memory with full user control, and a containment layer that prevents sensitive data from reaching external APIs. It's designed to be the foundation for ethical AI applications — personal assistants, healthcare tools, legal research, education — in any context where data sovereignty matters.

**Tech stack:** Rust · Tauri · React · PostgreSQL + pgvector · llama.cpp · Candle (BERT/Whisper)

The architecture extends beyond a single device — ZynkSync syncs your memory across your own machines over your local network, ZChat enables encrypted peer-to-peer messaging between paired devices, and ZynkLink lets you transfer files and LLM models directly between devices without an internet connection. Download a model once, share it everywhere on your local network. No cloud intermediary at any layer.

## ContainAI

[ContainAI](https://containai.ai) is my company. The mission is straightforward: build AI infrastructure that serves users rather than exploiting them. The core belief is that the ownership of your data and the AI systems that process it should belong to you — not to a corporation or government whose business model depends on your behavioral profile.

The open-source project is licensed under AGPL-3.0. Commercial licensing is available for organizations that want to build proprietary applications on this foundation.

## What I Care About

- **Privacy as architecture, not policy** — containment built into the system, not bolted on
- **Transparency** — you can see what the AI knows about you, what it retrieved, and why
- **Ethical AI governance** — safety modes, contradiction detection, user-controlled memory
- **Offline-first resilience** — full functionality without an internet connection
